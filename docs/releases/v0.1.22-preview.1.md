# Bible Companion 0.1.22 preview

Adds 42 reviewed public-domain artworks linked to specific Old and New Testament scenes, bringing the registered catalog to 43 works after the accompanying database migration. Each card retains its museum source, artist/date, credit and rights information. Japanese descriptions distinguish the artwork's interpretation from the biblical text; later scene details stay hidden until their full boundary.

The image transport adds explicitly reviewed Met and Cleveland sources with constrained URLs, redirect refusal, cookie-free requests and an 8 MB download limit. Existing tab counts and selected-tab image loading remain in use.

Release candidate: 0.1.22/build23. Production database, Worker, release and SideStore rollout must follow docs/artwork/rollout.md. Physical SideStore installation is not yet verified.
